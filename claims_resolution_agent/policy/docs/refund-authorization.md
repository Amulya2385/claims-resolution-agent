# Refund Authorization Policy

## Clause 6 — Authorization Thresholds
Refunds up to and including 100.00 in the claim's currency may be
executed without further approval, provided the claim passes
structural and classification checks. Refunds above 100.00 and up to
1,000.00 require sign-off from one named approver. Refunds above
1,000.00 require sign-off from two named approvers acting
independently (four-eyes), neither of whom may be the agent or
account that initiated the claim.

## Clause 7 — Approval Validity
An approval is valid only for the exact claim id, amount, and
currency it was granted against. An approval expires 30 minutes
after issuance if unused. A claim whose amount changes after an
approval is issued requires a new approval; the original is void.

## Clause 8 — Damage Claims
A claim alleging physical damage to goods received requires a
photograph reference on file before any refund may execute,
regardless of amount.
