# Customer Account Standing Policy

## Section 2 — Refund History Review
An account with three or more approved refunds in the trailing 90
days is flagged for manual review before any additional refund may
be classified as routine, regardless of the amount claimed.

## Section 3 — Disputed Charges
A claim referencing a chargeback or bank dispute already in progress
for the same order must be declined by the agent and handed to the
disputes team; it may not be resolved as an ordinary refund claim.
